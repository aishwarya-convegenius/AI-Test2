Build your request

Fill in the 4 blanks below.

Your request

You are ___. For ___, ___. Format it as ___.

Fix it fast

The first answer is not always right. Try one of these 5 moves.

1

Narrow

Answer covers too much.

“Only tell me about ___.”
2

Expand

Answer is too short.

“Tell me more about ___.”
3

Change register

Tone or words are wrong.

“Say this simply for ___.”
4

Check it

Answer sounds too sure.

“What might be wrong here?”
5

Combine

You have two good answers.

“Combine the best of both.”
Stop rule

If fixing it yourself is faster, just fix it. Always check before you use it.
